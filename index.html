GURCADE Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy describes how GURCADE, an Android mobile game published by EGGS - Indie Dev, handles information. This policy should be reviewed whenever the game's SDKs or features change.

Game progress

GURCADE stores gameplay progress, settings, coins and upgrades locally on your device. This information is used to maintain your game experience. If cloud saving or sign-in is introduced, this policy will be updated before launch of those features.

Advertising and third-party services

GURCADE uses Unity LevelPlay mediation to display rewarded, interstitial and banner advertisements. Advertising technology providers may automatically process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, ad interactions, diagnostic information and device characteristics, subject to their own practices and applicable consent requirements. The actual processing depends on the ad networks enabled for the app and the user's settings.

For more information, see Unity's Privacy Policy. Other advertising partners' policies may also apply depending on the networks enabled.

Purposes and sharing

Information processed by advertising providers may be used to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, maintain security, and diagnose performance. Such providers may receive data directly from their SDKs. We do not sell in-game progress data ourselves.

Gameplay analytics

When gameplay analytics is enabled, GURCADE uses Unity Analytics to understand how the game is used and improve its modes, levels, features and player experience. Events may describe mode selection, Shop opening, gameplay starts and outcomes, play duration and scores, level completion and stars, Boost and power-up use, tutorial completion, control-layout selection, mission completion and reward claims, and advertisement flow and accepted rewarded-ad outcomes.

Unity Analytics also processes installation and user identifiers, session information, device and app information, and IP-related information as described in Unity's documentation. These records are not completely anonymous. Our custom gameplay events do not include your name, email address, payment details or the contents of your local game-save file.

Analytics information is processed by Unity Technologies to provide its Analytics service. For more information about Unity's processing and applicable rights, see Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Advertising processing by LevelPlay and its partners remains separate, as described above.

Where an applicable legal basis permits collection with notice and an opportunity to opt out, gameplay analytics may be on by default. Where prior consent is required, collection remains off until that consent has been obtained. We do not treat advertising consent as permission for gameplay analytics.

You can turn Share Gameplay Analytics off in Settings > Privacy & Analytics at any time. Turning it off stops further Analytics collection, but is not itself a request to delete previously collected records. It does not change your advertising privacy choices or remove local game progress.

You can separately select Request Analytics Data Deletion in that Settings page. This turns Analytics off and asks Unity's Analytics service to delete the relevant Analytics data. Acceptance of the request by the SDK is not confirmation that server deletion has finished. An offline request may be retried when connectivity returns. This action does not delete your local game progress or request deletion from advertising partners. For other privacy questions or requests, use the contact address below.

Choices

You can manage advertising permissions and privacy choices through available device settings and any consent controls provided within the game. Resetting or deleting local game data may remove locally saved progress. Where applicable, you can request information about or deletion of personal data by contacting the developer.

Children

GURCADE's intended audience and advertising configuration must comply with the age groups declared in Google Play Console. We do not knowingly request that players submit their names, addresses or other personal details in the game.

Security and retention

Locally stored progress remains on your device until removed through the game's functionality, clearing app data or uninstalling. Third-party service providers control their own retention periods as described in their privacy policies.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The date at the top will show the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email etaigamesstudio@gmail.com.
